    Vigil Directorate¶

    Vigil Directorate
    Trade Military surplus, rare parts
    Sells Rare parts, military surplus
    Buys Little, and on their terms
    Starts at Wary (-300)
    Met when Navigation — tier 2, Star tracker is repaired — it never answers a call to anyone
    At Friend Parts nobody else will sell

    A military directorate that watches this system and does not explain why. They have surplus to sell and little patience for a stranded student. Rare parts, if they come to like you.

    What it sells¶

    What a landed trader has in stock after a restock; a better band adds the rows below it. See Trading.

    From Wary — Magnet ×6 · Lens ×4

    From Cold — Dynamite ×10

    From Neutral — Motor ×6

    From Acquainted — Weapon Rack ×1 · Pad Turret Blueprint ×1

    From Trusted — Silver Reflector ×4 · Napalm ×300 · Guidance Module ×2

    From Ally — Microchip ×10 · Tungsten-core Warhead ×2 · Phase Emitter ×1 · Armoured Door ×2 · Isotope Generator ×1

    From Friend — Platinum Catalyst ×4 · Gyroscope ×2 · Extended Magazine ×1

    What it buys¶

    The kinds of thing it takes, and how it pays on each against the usual price:

    Kind of thing It pays
    Components +20%
    Explosives +10%
    Titanium Ingot +30%
    Platinum Ingot +30%

    Its contracts¶

    It offers contracts at a landed trader's terminal. How contracts work: Contracts.

    Talking to them¶

    A landed trader's terminal lets you talk to them: 8 topics, 4 of them only as they come to like you. What they say is for you to find out.
