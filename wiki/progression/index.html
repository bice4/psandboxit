    Getting started¶

    62 quests teach the game one step at a time; most open a station when they are done, and finishing them raises your rank. They come in chains — each below says what it follows.

    Salvage¶

    The first quest.

    Salvage¶

    The ship came down in one piece, nearly. One locker in it stayed shut through the crash - whatever survived is in there.

    1. Right-click the ship's locker and take what it holds. — have Material Manipulator; have Multitool

    The manipulator, the multitool, two tanks and a flashlight - fit it on the Character tab, and L lights the way.

    Dig In … Plate by Plate¶

    After Salvage.

    Dig In¶

    The Material Manipulator picks up loose material, but the ground is solid. Break it loose with the Multitool first, then gather what falls.

    1. Dig with the Multitool (2), then pick the dirt up with the Manipulator (1). — carry 20 cells of Loose Dirt

    Loose material goes wherever you put it down. The manipulator carries one material at a time.

    Fill a Tank¶

    Tanks hold loose material. Place one from a heavy slot and pour dirt in through the hopper on top.

    1. Place a Tank from a heavy slot. — build Tank
    2. Pour loose dirt into it through the hopper. — store 100 cells of Loose Dirt

    A tank keeps what you pour into it, and it travels with its contents.

    Take It With You¶

    A full tank goes back into a heavy slot, and the material goes with you. Building spends what your carried tanks hold.

    1. Pick an empty slot and click the tank to take it back. — have 100 cells of Loose Dirt

    Carried tanks are your materials: every build cost is paid from them.

    Before the Mine¶

    Ore is under the ground, and a shaft is a long way down in the dark. The ship's hold still has what a miners' shuttle carries for that: ladders and lamps.

    1. Right-click the Ship's Storage - the crates in the hold by the stern - and take the ladders and the lamps. — have 10× Wood Ladder; have 2× Lamp

    A ladder goes down a tile at a time, and a lamp lights the rock round it. Both are placed from the bar like any construction.

    A Ladder of Your Own¶

    Ten ladders reach ten metres, and the ore goes deeper. A Wood Ladder is made of wood chips: fell a tree with the Multitool, gather the chips, and build as many as the shaft needs.

    1. Fell a tree with the Multitool (2) and collect the Wood Chips in a tank. — have 20 cells of Wood Chips
    2. Open the hub (Tab): in the Catalogue, drag a Wood Ladder to the bar - it is built there - then place it. — build 2× Wood Ladder

    A construction made of loose material is built this way: the material in a carried tank, then a drag to the bar.

    Build a Furnace¶

    A furnace turns ore into ingots. It is built from loose clay and loose dirt - dig both, collect each in a tank of its own, and place the furnace.

    1. Collect loose clay and loose dirt, each in a tank. — have 144 cells of Loose Clay; have 60 cells of Loose Dirt
    2. Place a Furnace. — build Furnace

    Opens: Furnace

    The furnace smelts ore into ingots. Open it and give it ore and fuel.

    First Melt¶

    The furnace fires clay into brick and smelts ore into ingots. A workbench needs both - and after it, the real making starts.

    1. Fire bricks and smelt iron in the furnace. — have 8× Brick; have 2× Iron Ingot
    2. Place a Workbench. — build Workbench

    Opens: Workbench

    The workbench forges plates, pipes and bearings from ingots.

    Plate by Plate¶

    Every machine of the camp starts with iron plate, and many with a bearing. Forge a stock of both at the workbench.

    1. Forge iron plates and bearings at the workbench. — have 6× Iron Plate; have 2× Bearing

    Two roads open from here: the soil and the metal. Follow either - or both.

    Locker Two¶

    After Plate by Plate.

    Locker Two¶

    The second locker in the ship's hold took a dent and will not open. Iron plate and copper wire would put it right, both made at the workbench. What is inside is worth it.

    1. Repair the second locker at the console by the hatch, then right-click it and take what it holds. — have Flamethrower

    A flamethrower and a wrench: fire to dry what is wet, and machines that can be moved.

    Air Works¶

    After Plate by Plate.

    Air Works¶

    Every ingot the furnace pours is carried to the workbench by hand. Aero tubes carry it by air from one machine's port to the next. The aero workshop makes the tubes, intakes and valves, all out of iron.

    1. Build an Aero Workshop from hull plates, iron pipe and bearings made at the workbench. — build Aero Workshop

    Opens: Aero Workshop

    The workshop is up. Next, the tubes it makes - and something to blow down them.

    Tubes and a Fan … Into the Silo¶

    After Air Works.

    Tubes and a Fan¶

    A tube carries what something blows into it. An intake is a fan in a hood: set it by loose ground and it pulls the grains in and sends them down the tube.

    1. Make a Machine Fan at the workbench - a bearing and fan blades. — have Machine Fan
    2. At the Aero Workshop make an Aero Intake from the fan, iron and copper ingots, and eight Aero Tubes from iron pipe. — have Intake; have 8× Aero Tube

    Eight tubes and an intake. All that is missing is somewhere for it to go.

    Into the Silo¶

    A Grain Silo holds loose ground by the thousand cells and takes it in at the top. Run the intake's tube to it and the digging carries itself home.

    1. Make a Grain Silo at the Aero Workshop - hull plates and glass, smelted from sand in the furnace - and set it down. — stand Grain Silo
    2. Set the intake on loose ground - sand, dirt - and lay tubes from its port to the silo's intake on top. Let it run. — deliver 200× Grain Silo

    The ground comes in by itself now. Every machine with a port can be fed the same way.

    Running Water¶

    After Into the Silo.

    Running Water¶

    Carrying water by the tank is slow. The pipe workshop makes pipes and pumps: put a pump in water and pipe it to where it is needed.

    1. Place a Pipe Workshop. — build Pipe Workshop
    2. Lay a pump and a run of pipe. — build Pump; build 4× Pipe

    Opens: Pipe Workshop

    A pump in a pool and a pipe to a station: water that comes by itself.

    Full Circle … Garden Under Glass¶

    After Running Water.

    Full Circle¶

    Nothing here goes to waste. A composter rots leaves and petals into biomass - the seed stock of a garden, the same that mown grass gives - and biomass and ash into loose dirt.

    1. Place a Composter. — build Composter
    2. Cut grass and gather the biomass it leaves. — have 20 cells of Biomass

    Opens: Composter

    Biomass and water are all a plant needs - given somewhere to grow.

    Garden Under Glass¶

    An incubator grows wood, leaves and flowers from biomass and water. Pipe the water in.

    1. Place a Plant Incubator. — build Plant Incubator
    2. Grow wood in it. — have 40 cells of Wood Chips

    Opens: Plant Incubator

    Grown wood, grown petals: the garden pays back what it is given.

    Grind It Down¶

    After Plate by Plate.

    Grind It Down¶

    A crusher grinds coal and iron to dust, petals to dye and wood to paper.

    1. Place a Crusher. — build Crusher
    2. Crush coal and iron to dust. — have 4× Coal Dust; have 4× Iron Dust

    Opens: Crusher

    Iron dust and coal dust, mixed, are the start of steel.

    Mix It Up¶

    After Grind It Down.

    Mix It Up¶

    A mixer tumbles dusts together. Iron and coal make steel dust; sulphur and coal make gunpowder.

    1. Place a Mixer. — build Mixer
    2. Mix iron dust and coal dust into steel dust. — have 10 cells of Steel Dust

    Opens: Mixer

    Steel dust smelts into steel in the furnace.

    Walls and Doors¶

    After Plate by Plate.

    Walls and Doors¶

    A camp needs walls that stay where they are put. The fabricator makes walls, ramps, ladders and doors.

    1. Place a Fabricator. — build Fabricator
    2. Make a door and set it in a wall. — build Iron Door

    Opens: Fabricator

    Walls keep the loose ground out - and doors let you back in.

    Coloured Light¶

    After Garden Under Glass, Grind It Down.

    Coloured Light¶

    Lilac flowers grow on the surface. Ground to dust in the crusher, a petal tints glass at the workbench - and tinted glass in a lamp colours its light.

    1. Tint a pane of glass with lilac petal dust. — have Lilac Glass
    2. Place a lamp and fit the glass from its panel. — build Lamp

    Any petal tints glass. The night here does not have to be one colour.

    Steel¶

    After Mix It Up, Running Water, Garden Under Glass, Into the Silo.

    Steel¶

    Steel is what the rest of the base is built from. Smelt steel dust into ingots in the furnace and forge plates at the workbench.

    1. Forge steel plates. — have 4× Steel Plate

    With steel, the rest of the ship's manifest can be built.

    More Room¶

    After Steel.

    More Room¶

    A chest holds one row. A storage module in one of its upgrade slots opens another, up to three modules a chest. The Ship's Storage takes modules the same way.

    1. Make a storage module at the workbench from steel plates and bearings. — have Storage Module
    2. Put it in an upgrade slot - a chest's, or the Ship's Storage's. — fit Storage Module

    Each module opens a row of eight. It comes out again only while that row is empty.

    Locker Three¶

    After Steel.

    Locker Three¶

    The third locker, in the crew deck, burnt in the crash. Steel plate and a pane of glass would make it whole again.

    1. Repair the third locker at the console by the hatch, then right-click it and take what it holds. — have Sprayer

    The Background Wall and the sprayer: walls behind the machines you build, and liquid where you aim it. And a ruler, to count the tiles between two places before you build across them.

    Clean Water¶

    After Steel.

    Clean Water¶

    Water from the caves, and from the washer, is dirty. A purifier filters it clean through a cartridge of coal dust and paper.

    1. Place a Water Purifier. — build Water Purifier
    2. Pack filter cartridges at the workbench. — have 2× Filter Cartridge

    Opens: Water Purifier

    Clean water: for the garden, the washer and the machines to come.

    Pan for Ore¶

    After Steel.

    Pan for Ore¶

    Washed with water, any common ground gives up a little ore concentrate - the raw stock of everything rare.

    1. Place a Washer. — build Washer
    2. Wash ground into ore concentrate. — have 20 cells of Ore Concentrate

    Opens: Washer

    Concentrate holds more than the veins show. Something will have to sort it.

    Thinking Machines¶

    After Clean Water, Pan for Ore.

    Thinking Machines¶

    Silicon ore smelts into wafers in the furnace; the assembler makes wafers and copper into chips, and chips, coils and plate into motors.

    1. Place an Assembler. — build Assembler
    2. Assemble microchips and a motor. — have 2× Microchip; have Motor

    Opens: Assembler

    Chips and motors: the last machines on the manifest can be built.

    Every Grain Its Place¶

    After Air Works, Thinking Machines.

    Every Grain Its Place¶

    One tube carries whatever is put into it, all mixed together. A sorter reads what passes and sends each material and each item down the side that asked for it. It takes microchips and a display, so it waited for the assembler.

    1. Make a Sorter at the Aero Workshop and place it on a tube line. — build Sorter

    One line in, three lines out, each with its own cargo: the camp sorts itself.

    Listening … A Place to Land¶

    After Thinking Machines.

    Listening¶

    The mast on the ship's hump was sheared off in the crash. Copper wire and a pair of microchips would put it back - and with it, an ear on the sky.

    1. Repair the antenna - Navigation's first tier - at the ship's console. — repair Navigation

    Someone is out there: traders' beacons, and a call sign for anyone with somewhere to land.

    A Place to Land¶

    A trader's lander needs flat ground it can trust under open sky. A landing pad gives it that, and holds the cargo you mean to trade.

    1. Place a Merchant Landing Pad under open sky. — build Merchant Landing Pad

    Opens: Merchant Landing Pad

    The pad stands ready. Its panel will call a trader as soon as one answers.

    Under Pressure¶

    After Thinking Machines.

    Under Pressure¶

    A compressor packs loose material into blocks - a tank's worth carried in one slot.

    1. Place a Compressor. — build Compressor
    2. Compress iron ore into blocks. — have 2× Compressed Iron

    Opens: Compressor

    Packed tight, a block travels light.

    Sorting the Grains¶

    After Thinking Machines.

    Sorting the Grains¶

    Ore concentrate holds more than stone. A separator sorts it grain by grain - mostly stone, sometimes copper or silicon, rarely gold or platinum.

    1. Place a Separator. — build Separator
    2. Sort concentrate until you have silicon. — have 10 cells of Raw Silicon

    Opens: Separator

    Keep it running. The rarest grains come one in a thousand draws, or fewer.

    Set in Stone¶

    After Thinking Machines.

    Set in Stone¶

    Sand, stone and ash with water make concrete. Pour it wet into a form of walls and let it set; broken up, it gives loose concrete back.

    1. Place a Concrete Mixer. — build Concrete Mixer
    2. Pour concrete, let it set, and break it loose. — have 20 cells of Concrete Rubble

    Opens: Concrete Mixer

    Loose concrete becomes walls, ramps and ladders at the fabricator.

    Instruments … Refinery¶

    After Thinking Machines.

    Instruments¶

    The suit has slots for instruments the ship no longer carries. The gadget bench makes them - start with the two that read the ground.

    1. Place a Gadget Bench. — build Gadget Bench
    2. Make a thermometer and a depth gauge. — have Thermometer; have Depth Gauge

    Opens: Gadget Bench

    Deep down, where the rock is hot, something lies that burns.

    By the Canister¶

    A liquid will not go into a tank you carry. A canister holds a hundred cells of it: a filler takes liquid from a pipe and an empty canister and gives a full one, and an extractor pours a full one back into a pipe.

    1. Place a Canister Filler and pipe water into it. — build Canister Filler
    2. Make a canister at the workbench. — have Empty Canister
    3. Put the canister into the filler and fill it with water. — have Water Canister
    4. Place a Canister Extractor, with a pipe from it to a liquid tank. — build Canister Extractor
    5. Put the full canister into the extractor and empty it. — run Canister Extractor

    Opens: Canister Filler, Canister Extractor

    Any liquid, and any gas, can now be carried by hand. Deep down, something waits to be carried.

    Black Gold¶

    Bitumen lies deep, in the hot layers. Heated past its melting point it runs as oil. Gather it in a liquid tank, and fill a canister to carry it up.

    1. Melt bitumen into oil, pipe it to a Canister Filler and fill a canister. — have Oil Canister

    Oil burns - and distilled, it gives something cleaner.

    Refinery¶

    A still distils oil into fuel, and gives off methane as it does. Fuel burns hot in any furnace, and it can be carried in canisters.

    1. Place a Still. — build Still
    2. Distil oil into fuel and store it in a liquid tank. — store 50 cells of Fuel

    Opens: Still

    Fuel in the tank, methane off the top. Both burn - and the methane can be put to work.

    Plastics … Locker Four¶

    After Refinery.

    Plastics¶

    The still's methane need not be wasted. Over a platinum catalyst, hot, it joins into plastic - and plastic insulates the wiring the finest instruments need.

    1. Place a Polymeriser and pipe methane into it. — build Polymeriser
    2. Make plastic, and insulate wire with it at the assembler. — have 2× Insulated Wire

    Opens: Polymeriser

    Insulated wire: the instruments and the suit's last upgrades take it.

    Locker Four¶

    The fourth locker, up in the hump over the crew deck, took a blow that crushed its corner and killed the chip in its lock. New microchips will wake it, and insulated wire - plastic over copper, which the assembler makes now there is plastic.

    1. Repair the fourth locker at the console by the hatch, then right-click it and take what it holds. — have Designator

    Opens: Electrics Bench

    The designator: mark the ground, and it is cleared for you. And a Wire Tool - with an Electrics Bench to build what the wires will join.

    Gas Lines¶

    After Refinery.

    Gas Lines¶

    The still gives off methane as it parts the oil. Gas runs through the same pipes as water, but it needs a pump of its own to draw it and a tank of its own to hold it.

    1. Place a Gas Pump and a Gas Tank. — build Gas Pump; build Gas Tank
    2. Pipe the still's methane into the gas tank. — store 50 cells of Methane

    Opens: Scrubber

    Methane in a tank, under pressure. Anything that burns clean can burn this.

    Split Water … Tanks Full¶

    After Gas Lines, Locker Four.

    Split Water¶

    Pure water, pulled apart in an electrolyser, gives hydrogen, the lightest fuel there is, and oxygen beside it. It is power that pulls it apart: the electrolyser burns nothing and stands still until it is wired to a battery bank or a panel.

    1. Place an Electrolyser and wire it to a Battery Bank with the Wire Tool. — build Electrolyser
    2. Split water and store the hydrogen in a gas tank. — store 50 cells of Hydrogen

    Opens: Electrolyser, Condenser, Electric Furnace, Boiler, Steam Turbine

    Hydrogen, from nothing but water. The ship's tank has been waiting for exactly this.

    Blue Flame¶

    A furnace on gas runs twice as fast. With oxygen piped in underneath it runs faster still, hot enough to turn iron ore and coal straight into steel.

    1. Place an Advanced Furnace. Pipe methane or hydrogen into its left inlet and oxygen into the inlet underneath. — build Advanced Furnace
    2. Pipe the electrolyser's oxygen into a gas tank - the furnace draws its oxygen from there. — store 50 cells of Oxygen
    3. Make steel from iron ore and coal in oxygen. — have 10× Steel Ingot

    Opens: Advanced Furnace, Autoclave, Fuel Cell

    Steel straight from the ore, in a flame that is almost invisible.

    Tanks Full¶

    The ship's tanks take gas through the inlet on its hull. Hydrogen for the engine, oxygen to burn it with: pump both in until the tanks are full.

    1. Pump hydrogen into the ship's hydrogen tank - built once Fuel is repaired. — fill 2000 cells of Hydrogen
    2. Pump oxygen into the ship's oxygen tank - built once the Oxidiser is repaired. — fill 1000 cells of Oxygen

    Both tanks full. The engine has everything it needs but a way off the ground.

    Sap and Sulphur … Leap of Faith¶

    After Refinery.

    Sap and Sulphur¶

    The ship's fuel lines and air loop need seals that give. Resin comes from felled trunks now and then, or grown in the incubator from wood, biomass and water; sulphur lies in the yellow veins.

    1. Gather resin and sulphur, each in a tank. — have 20 cells of Resin; have 10 cells of Sulphur

    Resin and sulphur, fired together, make rubber.

    Vulcanised¶

    The furnace takes two inputs: resin in one, sulphur in the other. Fired together they cure into rubber.

    1. Cure resin and sulphur into rubber in the furnace. — have 4× Rubber

    Rubber seals the airlocks, and makes a mat that springs.

    Leap of Faith¶

    A rubber mat on four springs throws you back a little higher than you fell, up to five tiles. Jump as you land for a little more; hold S to stop.

    1. Build a Trampoline and jump on it. — build Trampoline

    Opens: Trampoline

    Up is a direction again.

    Precious Metals … Locker Five¶

    After Refinery.

    Precious Metals¶

    Gold, silver and titanium lie deep, or come one grain in hundreds from the separator. The ship's last repairs need all three - and so do the best instruments.

    1. Smelt a gold, a silver and a titanium ingot. — have Gold Ingot; have Silver Ingot; have Titanium Ingot

    Gold for contacts, silver for mirrors, titanium for the suit.

    Locker Five¶

    Debris went through the tall locker in the hold. Nickel steel to patch it and a catalyst for its seal - the last of the ship's lockers.

    1. Repair the fifth locker at the console by the hatch, then right-click it and take what it holds. — have Rocket Launcher

    The rocket launcher and the jetpack. Every locker in the ship is open.

    Open Every Locker¶

    After Salvage.

    Open Every Locker¶

    The ship is the way off this planet. Everything broken in it has to be put right - its lockers first, its systems after.

    1. Repair every locker in the ship and take what each holds. — have Flamethrower; have Wrench; have Designator; have Rocket Launcher

    Every locker is open. The ship's systems are next.

    Hands Off¶

    The first quest.

    Hands Off¶

    Deepcut's licence is paid for. Their drones dig and carry what you point at, so the hours you spent on a slope can go to something else. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Build the Drone Station from the catalogue. — stand Drone Station
    2. Mark loose ground or a pool in the station's range with the Designator. Its collector brings it in. — haul 200× Drone Station
    3. Buy a Miner Drone from Deepcut and put it into the station's cargo. It breaks the solid ground you mark. — dock Miner Drone

    A miner to break the rock, a collector to bring it in: the ground you mark comes to the station by itself.

    Skin of the Ship¶

    The first quest.

    Skin of the Ship¶

    The roof is holed, the glass is cracked, the bow is gone. The hull is repaired at the console by the hatch, a layer at a time, and each layer wants a better plate than the last.

    1. Patch the roof with hull plate and wire: repair the Hull at the console by the hatch. — repair Hull
    2. Strap the glass with steel plate and insulated wire. — repair 2× Hull
    3. Close the breach with plasteel and nickel-steel plate. — repair 3× Hull
    4. Build the nose from composite panels: carbon fibre cured in resin in the autoclave. — repair 4× Hull

    Composite over plasteel over steel: the hull will hold what the climb puts on it.

    The survey dish came off its yoke in the crash and lies on the hold's roof. It was the miners' way of reading the rock under the ship. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    Head of Steam¶

    The first quest.

    Head of Steam¶

    Panels sleep at night, and an arc furnace eats more than a roof of them gives. A boiler turns water and anything that burns into steam; a turbine turns the steam into power and hands the water back. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Place a Boiler and a Steam Turbine. Pipe the boiler's dome to the turbine's inlet, and the turbine's outlet back to the boiler's. — build Boiler; build Steam Turbine

    Deep Heat¶

    The first quest.

    Deep Heat¶

    Hydrogen and oxygen made the ship's fuel; across a silver membrane they make power instead, with no flame and water left over. The ground is warm for nothing wherever lava lies under it. And a night is long for an arc furnace. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Place a Fuel Cell. Pipe hydrogen into its left and oxygen into its top, and its water out of the right. — build Fuel Cell
    2. Make a Geothermal Tap at the Electrics Bench and stand it on rock over lava. The Visor shows how hot the ground under it is. — build Geothermal Tap
    3. Make an Accumulator at the Electrics Bench and wire it in. — build Accumulator

    A wire that is live at midnight, with no fire anywhere on it.

    Switched On¶

    The first quest.

    Switched On¶

    The Wire Tool has a second mode. In it a wire carries no power, only yes or no: from a lever to a door, from a sensor to a pump, through an element that says the opposite. A base that is wired this way runs itself. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Make a Signal Lever at the Electrics Bench and place it. With the Wire Tool in hand press R for signal wires, click the lever and then a door or a lamp, and throw the lever with the right button. — build Signal Lever
    2. Place a Daylight Sensor and a NOT Element. Wire the sensor to the element and the element to a lamp: it lights at dusk. — build Daylight Sensor; build NOT Element
    3. Place a Fill Sensor near the top of a pit or a bin and wire it to whatever fills it, through a NOT Element: it stops when the bin is full. — build Fill Sensor

    The door answers the lever, the lamp answers the dark, and nobody has to stand there.

    Keep a Stock¶

    The first quest.

    Keep a Stock¶

    A chest can say how much it holds. Wired to a station, it starts the work when the shelf runs low and stops it when the shelf is full. The datalog's Signal Schemes shows how. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Feed a Furnace by tube and let it put its ingots into a Chest. With the Wire Tool in signal mode, right-click the chest and set output A to "holds fewer than" a number of ingots, then wire A to the furnace. — wire Chest
    2. Let it run: the furnace smelts until the chest holds the number, and starts again when you take some out. — run 5× Furnace

    The furnace works when the chest is short and rests when it is not. Nobody counts the ingots any more.

    Supply on Demand¶

    The first quest.

    Supply on Demand¶

    A silo with a wire on its input gives to the tubes only while the signal is on. A station can give that signal itself, when it has nothing left to work on. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Set the Furnace's output A to "lacks input" and wire it to the input of the Grain Silo that feeds it. — wire Furnace
    2. Set its output B to "works" and wire it to a Lamp: it lights while the furnace is busy. — wire Furnace

    The silo sends ore only when the furnace asks for it, and a lamp tells you when it is running.

    Exactly Forty¶

    After Air Works.

    Exactly Forty¶

    A Counter Tube set to Stop lets a number through and no more until it is reset. A button on its reset measures out a batch at a press, and a display shows how far the batch has got. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Make a Counter Tube at the Aero Workshop and put it into an aero line. In its panel set the number and choose Stop. — build Counter Tube
    2. Wire a Signal Button to the counter's reset, and the counter to a Text Display with {n} in its text. — wire Signal Button; wire Counter Tube

    A press, forty, and the line stands. The display says when the batch is whole.

    Power to Spare¶

    The first quest.

    Power to Spare¶

    The Wire Tool lay in the fourth locker for a reason. Silicon cells turn daylight into power, a bank of cells keeps it through the night, and a machine wired to them works faster for as long as there is charge. Nothing here is needed to leave the planet.

    1. Wind two Wire Spools at the assembler: four insulated wires each. — have 2× Wire Spool
    2. Place a Solar Panel under an open sky and a Battery Bank within 16 m of it. With the Wire Tool in hand, click the panel and then the bank. — build Solar Panel; build Battery Bank

    The bank's lamps climb through the day. Whatever is wired to it runs faster while they are lit.
